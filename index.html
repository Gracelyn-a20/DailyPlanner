Privacy Policy for DailyPlanner

Last updated: October 25, 2026  ·  Developer: DailyPlanner

Summary DailyPlanner does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Introduction

DailyPlanner ("the App," "we," "us") is a productivity and planning application. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

DailyPlanner is designed to work locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers or to any third parties.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Any information you enter into the App — such as your tasks, plans, notes, and reminders — stays only on your device. It is not sent off your device, and we cannot access it.

If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this information will be deleted and cannot be recovered.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share any user data with third parties. The App does not include third-party advertising networks, analytics providers, or any SDKs that collect data.

5. Permissions

The App may request certain permissions to function properly (for example, notifications for reminders). These permissions are used only for the App's functionality and not to collect data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. Since we collect no personal information, the App is safe for all ages.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with a new "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
